# Skill Development and Training Policy
**Document ID:** POL-SKILL-008  
**Version:** 1.3  
**Effective Date:** January 2025  
**Owner:** HR & Learning Development Team

---

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines how skill gaps are identified, addressed, and tracked within the ePARS system. It governs training allocation, mandatory learning requirements, and how skill data feeds into task assignment and team formation decisions.

---

## 2. Skill Proficiency Levels

| Level | Code | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Novice | 1 | Aware of the skill; requires supervision for all tasks |
| Beginner | 2 | Can perform basic tasks with guidance |
| Intermediate | 3 | Works independently on standard tasks |
| Advanced | 4 | Works independently on complex tasks; can guide others |
| Expert | 5 | Deep mastery; considered an organizational authority |

---

## 3. Skill Gap Identification
- The system must perform a **skill gap analysis** whenever a new project or task is created, comparing required skills against the current workforce profile.
- Individual skill gap reports must be generated for every employee **once per quarter**, aligned with the PEM evaluation cycle.
- Skills that are marked as **mandatory for the employee's role** and rated below Level 3 are considered critical gaps.

---

## 4. Training Allocation Rules

### 4.1 Mandatory Training
- Employees with a critical skill gap (mandatory skill at Level 1 or 2) must be enrolled in relevant training within **20 business days** of gap identification.
- Compliance and security training is mandatory for all employees and must be completed annually.
- Failure to complete mandatory training within the deadline reduces the employee's task assignment eligibility for non-critical skills.

### 4.2 Recommended Training
- The system must generate personalized training recommendations for each employee based on:
  - Current skill gaps relative to their career path.
  - Skills most in demand across active projects.
  - Skills held by high-performing employees in the same role.
- Recommended training has no deadline but contributes positively to PEM scores when completed.

### 4.3 Training Load Limits
- Employees enrolled in training must have their **available workload capacity reduced** by the estimated training hours.
- No employee should be enrolled in training requiring more than **8 hours/week** while simultaneously carrying a full task workload.
- Employees on burnout leave (Wellness Leave) must not be enrolled in any training programs during their leave period.

---

## 5. Skill Verification
- Skills claimed by employees must be verified through one of the following:
  - Completion of an internal skill assessment.
  - External certification upload.
  - Manager validation for experiential/on-the-job skills.
- Unverified skills are flagged in the system and may not be used for task assignment or team formation until verified.
- Skills must be re-verified every **2 years** to ensure currency.

---

## 6. Impact on Task Assignment
- Verified skill level directly influences task assignment eligibility:
  - Level 1–2: Not eligible for tasks requiring that skill unless as a training assignment.
  - Level 3: Eligible for standard tasks requiring that skill.
  - Level 4–5: Eligible for all tasks including high-complexity and P1 tasks requiring that skill.
- The system must always display which skill levels are being matched when recommending an employee for a task.

---

## 7. Knowledge Transfer Obligations
- Employees at Level 4 or 5 in any skill are expected to conduct at least **one knowledge-sharing session per quarter** for team members at lower levels.
- Knowledge transfer sessions must be logged in the system and contribute positively to the collaboration score component of the PEM model.
