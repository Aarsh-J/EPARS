# Performance Evaluation Policy
**Document ID:** POL-PERF-004  
**Version:** 3.0  
**Effective Date:** January 2025  
**Owner:** HR Department

---

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines how employee performance is evaluated, how PEM (Performance Evaluation Model) scores are interpreted, and what actions must follow specific score ranges. It ensures transparent, data-driven, and fair performance management.

---

## 2. Performance Score Bands

| Band | Score Range | Label | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exceptional | 90–100 | Platinum | Consistently exceeds all expectations |
| High Performer | 75–89 | Gold | Regularly exceeds expectations |
| Meets Expectations | 60–74 | Silver | Consistently meets role requirements |
| Developing | 50–59 | Bronze | Partially meets expectations; improvement needed |
| Underperforming | Below 50 | Red | Does not meet minimum expectations |

---

## 3. Evaluation Cycle
- Performance is evaluated on a **quarterly basis** using the PEM model.
- Mid-cycle assessments may be triggered if an employee's performance indicators change significantly (±15 points from last score).
- Annual reviews aggregate the four quarterly scores and compute a weighted average (most recent quarter weighted at 40%, others at 20% each).

---

## 4. Mandatory Actions by Score Band

### 4.1 Exceptional (90–100)
- Employee must be nominated for the quarterly **Star Performer recognition program**.
- Must be considered for **promotion eligibility review** if they have held their current role for ≥ 12 months.
- Eligible for **stretch assignments** and **leadership pipeline** programs.
- May be assigned as a **mentor** to Bronze or developing employees.

### 4.2 High Performer (75–89)
- Eligible for all task types and team lead roles.
- Should be considered for upskilling opportunities aligned with their career development plan.
- Manager must conduct a **growth-focused 1:1** at least once per quarter.

### 4.3 Meets Expectations (60–74)
- Eligible for standard task assignments.
- Manager should review career development goals once per quarter.
- If the employee's score remains in this band for **3 consecutive quarters**, a development plan must be jointly created.

### 4.4 Developing (50–59)
- Employee must be placed on a **Structured Development Plan (SDP)** within 10 business days of score publication.
- Task assignments must be limited to **medium and low priority (P3/P4)**.
- A monthly 1:1 check-in with manager is mandatory.
- A mentor (PEM ≥ 75) must be assigned within 15 business days.
- Must be re-evaluated after **6 weeks** to track improvement.

### 4.5 Underperforming (Below 50)
- Employee must be placed on a formal **Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)** within 5 business days.
- No new task assignments without explicit manager and HR approval.
- Bi-weekly review meetings with manager and HR are mandatory.
- PIP duration is 90 days with defined measurable milestones.
- If PEM score does not improve to ≥ 50 within the PIP period, escalation to senior HR is required.
- Employee must not be assigned to client-facing tasks or as a team lead during an active PIP.

---

## 5. Score Calculation Inputs
The PEM model uses the following key inputs (managed by the ML pipeline):

- **Historical performance score** — rolling weighted average of past evaluations.
- **Task completion rate** — percentage of assigned tasks completed on time.
- **Quality score** — peer and manager assessment of work output.
- **Collaboration score** — 360-degree input from teammates.
- **Skill utilization rate** — degree to which the employee's skills were applied to assigned tasks.
- **Training completion** — percentage of mandatory and recommended training completed.
- **Late delivery rate** — proportion of tasks delivered past their deadline.

---

## 6. Transparency and Appeals
- Every employee has the right to view their PEM score and the key features that influenced it.
- Employees may file a **score appeal** within 10 business days of score publication.
- Appeals are reviewed by a panel of HR and one neutral department representative.
- A valid appeal must provide evidence that one or more input data points were incorrect.
- The maximum allowable score adjustment via appeal is **±10 points**.
- Approved adjustments must be applied to the next cycle's score history.

---

## 7. Data Usage Restrictions
- PEM scores must not be used for disciplinary action without corroborating qualitative evidence from the manager.
- Scores must not be shared externally or included in references without explicit employee consent.
- Historical scores older than **3 years** must be archived and excluded from active evaluation calculations.

---

## 8. Score Volatility Policy
- A single-cycle drop of **≥ 20 points** must trigger an automatic system flag and manager review.
- A single-cycle drop of **≥ 30 points** must trigger an HR review in addition to manager review.
- Sudden spikes of **≥ 20 points** must also be reviewed to ensure data integrity.
