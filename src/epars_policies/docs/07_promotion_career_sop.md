# Promotion and Career Progression Standard Operating Procedure
**Document ID:** SOP-CAREER-007  
**Version:** 1.1  
**Effective Date:** January 2025  
**Owner:** HR Department

---

## 1. Purpose
This SOP outlines the criteria and process for employee promotions and career progression decisions. It defines how PEM scores, tenure, skill levels, and leadership history factor into promotion eligibility.

---

## 2. Promotion Eligibility Criteria

### 2.1 Performance Requirements
- The employee must have maintained a PEM score of **≥ 75 for at least 2 consecutive quarters** prior to the promotion cycle.
- No active Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) may be in place at the time of nomination.
- The employee must not have had a PEM score below 60 in any of the **last 4 evaluation cycles**.

### 2.2 Tenure Requirements
- Minimum **12 months** in the current role before promotion eligibility.
- Promotions to senior roles require a minimum of **18 months** in the current role.
- Promotions to leadership/management roles require a minimum of **24 months** in the current role plus evidence of team lead experience.

### 2.3 Skill and Competency Requirements
- The employee must have completed **at least 80%** of the competency requirements defined for the target role.
- At least **2 skill certifications** or equivalent internal assessments relevant to the target role must be on record.
- For leadership roles: the employee must have led at least **2 completed projects** as Team Lead with a team size of ≥ 3.

### 2.4 Behavioral Criteria
- Collaboration score (from 360-degree feedback) must be **≥ 7.0/10**.
- No formal complaints upheld against the employee in the **last 12 months**.
- Evidence of mentoring or knowledge-sharing activities is strongly preferred.

---

## 3. Promotion Process
1. **System Flag** — ePARS automatically identifies eligible employees at the start of each promotion cycle (bi-annual: June and December).
2. **Manager Nomination** — Manager reviews system-flagged employees and submits nominations with justification within **10 business days**.
3. **HR Review** — HR validates eligibility criteria against system records within **5 business days**.
4. **Promotion Panel** — A panel of the Department Head, HR lead, and one peer representative reviews nominations.
5. **Decision Communication** — Decisions communicated to employees within **5 business days** of the panel meeting.
6. **System Update** — Approved promotions updated in the system with new role, salary band, and skill profile.

---

## 4. Declined Promotion Appeals
- Employees whose nominations are declined may appeal within **15 business days**.
- Appeals must include specific new information not considered in the original review.
- A second panel review will be conducted within **20 business days** of appeal submission.

---

## 5. Fast-Track Promotions
- Employees with 3 consecutive Exceptional (90+) PEM scores may be considered for fast-track promotion regardless of standard tenure requirements.
- Fast-track nominations require Department Head approval in addition to standard process.
- Minimum tenure for fast-track is **6 months** in the current role.
