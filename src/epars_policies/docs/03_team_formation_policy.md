# Team Formation Policy
**Document ID:** POL-TEAM-003  
**Version:** 1.7  
**Effective Date:** January 2025  
**Owner:** Project Management Office

---

## 1. Purpose
This policy governs how project teams are formed within the organization. It defines the criteria for team composition, skill balancing, workload distribution, and role assignment to ensure high team effectiveness and fair distribution of opportunities.

---

## 2. Team Composition Principles

### 2.1 Minimum Team Requirements
- Every project team must have a minimum of **2 members** and a maximum of **8 members** unless a project-specific exception is approved.
- Every team must designate exactly **one Team Lead**.
- Every team must have at least **one member with domain expertise** relevant to the project's primary objective.

### 2.2 Skill Coverage
- A team's combined skill profile must cover **at least 80%** of the skills required for the project.
- No single skill critical to the project (marked as mandatory) may have **zero coverage** in the team.
- Skill redundancy (more than one expert per critical skill) is encouraged for projects with a timeline exceeding **3 months**.

### 2.3 Performance Balance
- The average PEM score of the team must be **≥ 60**.
- Teams must not be composed entirely of employees with PEM scores below 65 — at least **one member with PEM ≥ 75** is required.
- No more than **30% of the team** may consist of employees currently on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).

---

## 3. Team Lead Selection Criteria
- The Team Lead must have a PEM score of **≥ 75**.
- The Team Lead must have a burnout score of **≤ 0.45** (Low to low-Medium range).
- The Team Lead must have **prior experience leading at least one completed project** or have completed the internal leadership readiness assessment.
- The Team Lead must have a current workload of **≤ 32 hours/week** to accommodate additional coordination responsibilities.
- The Team Lead's skill profile must cover the project's primary skill domain.

---

## 4. Workload Balance Rules
- At the time of team formation, no team member's projected total workload (existing + new project tasks) should exceed **40 hours/week**.
- The workload distribution across the team should be as uniform as possible; the difference between the highest and lowest workload member must not exceed **15 hours/week**.
- Employees with burnout scores above **0.55** must not be assigned to a new team unless no other suitable candidate exists.

---

## 5. Diversity and Fairness Guidelines
- Team formation must not be influenced by gender, age, religion, ethnicity, or other protected characteristics.
- The system must ensure that high-visibility, high-reward projects are not disproportionately assigned to the same group of top performers across multiple cycles.
- Employees who have not been assigned to any team in the **last 60 days** should be prioritized for inclusion in new teams, provided they meet the eligibility criteria.

---

## 6. Cross-Functional Teams
- Cross-functional teams (drawing from more than one department) are recommended for projects requiring diverse skill sets.
- When forming cross-functional teams, each department contributing members must have at least **one representative with domain expertise** in that department's area.
- Cross-functional team members must be cleared by their current project managers before being allocated to a new team.

---

## 7. Team Size Guidelines by Project Type

| Project Type | Recommended Team Size | Min Team Lead PEM |
|---|---|---|
| Research & Analysis | 2–4 | 70 |
| Software Development | 3–6 | 75 |
| Infrastructure / DevOps | 2–5 | 75 |
| Client-Facing Delivery | 4–8 | 80 |
| Internal Process Improvement | 2–4 | 65 |

---

## 8. Re-Teaming and Reassignment
- Employees may not be involuntarily moved from an active team unless their burnout score exceeds **0.75** or their PEM score drops below **45** for two consecutive evaluation cycles.
- Voluntary team transfers are subject to manager approval and must not disrupt active project delivery.
- When a team member leaves mid-project, the system must automatically identify a replacement candidate within **24 hours** using the same formation criteria.

---

## 9. Historical Performance Consideration
- The system should prefer team compositions where members have a **positive collaboration history** (measured by joint project success rates in past assignments).
- Members with a documented **conflict history** (two or more formal complaints involving each other) must not be placed on the same team without HR review.
- Historical team synergy scores, if available, must be factored into the formation algorithm.
