# Task Assignment Policy
**Document ID:** POL-TASK-001  
**Version:** 2.1  
**Effective Date:** January 2025  
**Owner:** HR & Project Management Office

---

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines the rules and criteria by which tasks are assigned to employees within the organization. It ensures fair, skill-aligned, and workload-balanced task distribution across all project teams.

---

## 2. Eligibility for Task Assignment

### 2.1 Performance Score Thresholds
- Employees with a Performance Evaluation Model (PEM) score **above 75** are eligible for high-priority or complex tasks.
- Employees with a PEM score **between 50 and 74** are eligible for medium-priority tasks with optional mentorship support.
- Employees with a PEM score **below 50** must not be assigned new tasks without prior manager approval and must be placed on a performance improvement plan (PIP).

### 2.2 Workload Limits
- No employee shall be assigned tasks that result in a projected weekly workload **exceeding 45 hours**.
- Employees currently assigned **3 or more active tasks** must not receive additional task assignments until at least one task is completed or reassigned.
- Employees on approved leave (partial or full) must not receive new task assignments during the leave period.

### 2.3 Skill Alignment
- Tasks must be assigned only to employees whose skill profiles contain **at least 60% match** with the required skill set for the task.
- Stretch assignments (40–59% skill match) are permitted for junior employees as part of a development plan, provided a senior mentor is assigned concurrently.
- Assignments below 40% skill match are strictly prohibited unless no other employee is available and the task is non-critical.

---

## 3. Priority-Based Assignment Rules

### 3.1 Critical Priority Tasks (P1)
- Must be assigned to employees with PEM score ≥ 80.
- Workload at time of assignment must be ≤ 30 hours/week.
- Burnout score must be **Low** (below 0.3).
- Requires manager confirmation before assignment is finalized.

### 3.2 High Priority Tasks (P2)
- Must be assigned to employees with PEM score ≥ 65.
- Workload at time of assignment must be ≤ 38 hours/week.
- Burnout score must be **Low or Medium** (below 0.6).

### 3.3 Medium Priority Tasks (P3)
- May be assigned to employees with PEM score ≥ 50.
- Workload at time of assignment must be ≤ 42 hours/week.
- Burnout score must be below 0.7.

### 3.4 Low Priority Tasks (P4)
- May be assigned to any eligible employee.
- Workload limit: ≤ 45 hours/week.
- Suitable for employees in recovery from medium burnout as a re-engagement mechanism.

---

## 4. Reassignment Rules
- A task may be reassigned if the assigned employee's burnout score crosses **0.75** after assignment.
- A task may be reassigned if there is a projected delay of **more than 3 business days** beyond the deadline and the employee's workload is above threshold.
- Reassignment must be logged in the system with a reason code.
- The original assignee must be notified and their workload updated immediately.

---

## 5. Conflict of Interest
- Employees must not be assigned tasks that involve direct evaluation of their own performance metrics.
- Managers must not exclusively assign high-visibility tasks to employees they directly mentor without system-validated justification.

---

## 6. Exceptions
- All exceptions to this policy require written approval from the Department Head and must be logged in the system within 24 hours.
- Emergency task assignments during critical incidents may bypass workload thresholds but must be reviewed within 48 hours.
