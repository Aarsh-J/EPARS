# Employee Burnout Prevention and Wellbeing Policy
**Document ID:** POL-WELL-002  
**Version:** 1.4  
**Effective Date:** January 2025  
**Owner:** HR Department & Employee Wellness Committee

---

## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes protocols to detect, prevent, and respond to employee burnout. It defines intervention thresholds based on Workload and Burnout Prediction (WBP) model outputs and outlines mandatory actions for each burnout severity level.

---

## 2. Burnout Classification

The WBP model classifies employees into three burnout levels based on a continuous score from 0.0 to 1.0:

| Burnout Level | Score Range | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Low | 0.00 – 0.39 | Green |
| Medium | 0.40 – 0.69 | Amber |
| High | 0.70 – 1.00 | Red |

---

## 3. Intervention Protocols by Burnout Level

### 3.1 Low Burnout (Score: 0.00 – 0.39)
- **Status:** Normal operations.
- No mandatory intervention required.
- Routine wellness check-in recommended every 4 weeks.
- Employee is fully eligible for all task assignments and team roles.

### 3.2 Medium Burnout (Score: 0.40 – 0.69)
- **Status:** Amber alert — proactive intervention required.
- The system must automatically flag the employee for manager review within **48 hours** of score crossing 0.40.
- Manager must conduct a 1:1 wellbeing check-in within **5 business days**.
- New task assignments must be limited to **low or medium priority (P3/P4)** only.
- Employee's weekly workload must be reduced to **≤ 35 hours** until score returns below 0.40.
- Discretionary leave of up to **2 days** must be offered if the score remains in amber for **more than 10 consecutive days**.
- Employees at medium burnout must NOT be assigned as team leads or given mentorship responsibilities.

### 3.3 High Burnout (Score: 0.70 – 1.00)
- **Status:** Red alert — immediate intervention mandatory.
- All pending new task assignments for this employee must be **immediately blocked** by the system.
- Manager must be notified **within 2 hours** of the score crossing 0.70.
- A mandatory meeting with HR must be scheduled within **2 business days**.
- Employee must be moved to a **reduced workload plan**: no more than **20 hours/week** of active task work.
- A minimum of **3 business days of wellness leave** must be offered.
- Employee must not be assigned to any new project teams until burnout score drops below 0.50.
- Existing task assignments must be reviewed and up to **50%** reassigned to other available employees.
- If score exceeds **0.90**, mandatory referral to the Employee Assistance Program (EAP) is required.

---

## 4. Key Burnout Indicators (WBP Model Features)
The following indicators are monitored by the WBP model and should inform managerial decisions:

- **Job Satisfaction Score** — below 3.0/10 is a critical signal.
- **Job Control Score** — low autonomy correlates strongly with burnout.
- **Late Hours Frequency** — employees logging late hours more than **3 times per week** for 2+ consecutive weeks must be reviewed.
- **Vacation Days Unused** — employees with **≥ 10 unused vacation days** in a quarter must be prompted to take leave.
- **Role Ambiguity Score** — high role ambiguity (score > 7/10) must be resolved through a role clarity session.
- **Stress Load Score** — engineered composite; above 0.65 triggers amber alert independently.
- **Pressure Index** — above 0.72 triggers review regardless of overall burnout score.

---

## 5. Confidentiality
- Burnout scores are confidential and accessible only to the employee, their direct manager, and HR.
- Burnout data must not be used in annual performance evaluations or promotion decisions.
- Burnout history must be purged from active records after **12 months** of the employee returning to low burnout status.

---

## 6. Manager Responsibilities
- Managers are responsible for acting on system alerts within the timeframes defined in Section 3.
- Failure to act on a High Burnout alert within 2 hours will trigger an automatic escalation to the Department Head.
- Managers must document all interventions in the system within 24 hours of the intervention taking place.

---

## 7. Re-Integration After High Burnout
- Employees returning from burnout-related leave must be onboarded back gradually.
- Week 1 post-return: maximum 20 hours of active work, P4 tasks only.
- Week 2 post-return: maximum 30 hours, P3/P4 tasks only.
- Week 3+ post-return: normal task eligibility restored if WBP score is below 0.40.
- A re-integration check-in with the manager is mandatory at the end of each return week.
